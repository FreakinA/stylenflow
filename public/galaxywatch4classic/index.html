The galaxy watch was released five years ago as along with other werabales from samsung as is their custom every year. It come in two variants, the 42mm ans 46mm.

Before it was released, there was alot of rumor with it shippig with wearOS which was a drastic shift from the all time Tizen OS samsung was familiar with. This was an opportunity for samsung and google to start from a clean slate especially after the previous attempts with Huawei Watch 2, LG G watch, moto 360 and many others had dramatically failed.

Turns out, the rumors were true with the galaxy watch 4 clssic launching with Android Wear OS 4 baked into 316l stainless steel frame and Gorilla glass DX to top it all off. For a smart watch enthusisat like me, the fun part hadn’t come yet. The rotating bezel has for a long time been my favorite feature on all galaxy watch classic model. So much so that I still have on my wrist up to this point.
